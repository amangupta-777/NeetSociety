How NEET Society works

A clear rhythm that helps turn advice into action—without adding another pile of classes to your schedule.

01

Meet your mentor

Join a personal WhatsApp group with your mentor and up to 10 mentees, where you can get guidance and discuss preparation with peers.

02

Set weekly goals

Break preparation into realistic priorities based on your class, coaching schedule, and current progress.

03

Check in and connect

Share progress, ask for guidance, and use agreed mentor availability for personal calls when needed.

04

Review and reset

Use the weekly Google Meet to discuss progress, test mistakes, backlogs, and the next week's plan.

Support for your stage

Wherever you are in your NEET journey

Students have different needs at different stages. Mentorship should reflect that.

Build the base

Class 11 aspirants

Build routines before small backlogs grow.

  • Keep up with current chapters
  • Develop revision and question-practice habits
  • Build a sustainable weekly routine
Balance and revise

Class 12 aspirants

Manage current syllabus alongside previous topics.

  • Plan Class 11 revision
  • Review PYQs and mock-test performance
  • Identify weak chapters early
Learn and improve

NEET droppers

Turn lessons from a previous attempt into a clearer plan.

  • Review previous preparation patterns
  • Address repeated mistakes
  • Build consistency and test-review habits
